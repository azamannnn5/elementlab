Care Guide

Store it right.

Follow these steps to protect what you ordered. Always check the label on your product too.

Peptides before reconstituting

Keep lyophilized vials in the freezer, between -20°C and -80°C.

Peptides after reconstituting

Refrigerate between 2°C and 8°C. Keep away from direct light. Never freeze reconstituted solutions.

Injectable vials

Store at room temperature, away from direct light and heat. Shake suspensions well before use. Do not freeze.

Tablets

Keep tablets in the original foil pouch until needed. Store in a cool, dry place away from light and moisture, and out of reach of children.

Bacteriostatic water

Store at room temperature, away from direct light. Discard a vial 28 days after it is first opened.
