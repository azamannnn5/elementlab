Peptide Reconstitution Calculator

Use this calculator to determine the correct volume to draw when reconstituting peptides with bacteriostatic water for your research.

!
For Research Purposes Only - Not for Human Consumption This calculator is provided as a research tool to assist with accurate reconstitution of lyophilised peptides in a laboratory setting. Enter your syringe size, peptide vial quantity, bacteriostatic water volume, and desired dosage to calculate the volume required per dose.

How to Use

  1. Enter the size of your syringe in ml (e.g., 1 ml, 0.5 ml)
  2. Enter the total peptide quantity in the vial in mg
  3. Enter the amount of bacteriostatic water you will add in ml
  4. Enter your desired dosage in either mg or mcg (fill one field)
  5. The calculator will display the volume to draw per dose and total doses per vial
