This Privacy Policy explains how Imperial Blowork may collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, or place an order.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect details such as your name, email address, phone number, billing or delivery information, order details, messages you send us, and technical information such as your device, browser, and website activity.

How Information Is Used

We may use information to process and manage orders, communicate with you, respond to questions, provide customer support, maintain website security, improve our services, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing and Storage

Information may be shared with service providers who help operate the website or fulfil services, such as payment, hosting, delivery, analytics, or customer-support providers. These parties should use information only as needed for their services. We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be retained for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here or as required by law.

Cookies and Security

The website may use cookies or similar technologies to support site functions, remember preferences, and understand usage. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although some features may not work as intended. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices and Policy Updates

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain uses. To make a request, contact Imperial Blowork using the details listed on the website. We may update this policy from time to time by posting a revised version.
